## Rules for Copilot

- Use these as approved **style and phrasing options**, not a fixed paragraph or a checklist requiring every detail.
- Synthesize a coherent student-specific Background Information section from one or several examples, eliminating repetition.
- Include therapist-supplied additional history: referral context, educational placement and services, relevant school transitions, functional concerns, previous interventions, and pertinent educational/developmental context, as supported by notes.
- Omit unknown details rather than printing placeholder tokens in the final report; ask the therapist for material gaps separately.
- Correct obvious template artifacts (such as `{.She,She}`) when filling pronouns. Avoid unnecessary gender labels.
- Do not insert assessment administration dates, scores, or detailed findings here.
- Do not reuse the example student's age, school, grade, or ACC placement for other students.
