## Clinical vocabulary reference — NOT approved boilerplate
These are **topic cues only**, drawn from the staff handbook. They must not be inserted as findings without evidence.

- Fine motor/manipulation: pincer grasp, bilateral coordination, translation, shift, rotation, grasp strength, tool use.
- Graphomotor/handwriting: formation, sizing, spacing, baseline alignment, reversals, writing pressure, endurance.
- Visual-motor/perceptual: copying, tracing, visual scanning, discrimination, figure-ground, visual closure.
- Sensory: Sensory Profile 2 patterns (Seeking, Avoiding, Sensitivity, Registration) and relevant domains; SPM-2 domains (Vision, Hearing, Touch, Taste/Smell, Body Awareness, Balance and Motion, Planning and Ideas, Social Participation).
- Adaptive/functional: dressing, feeding, hygiene, materials management, transitions, task persistence and classroom participation.

## Approved assessment descriptions
Retrieve the *actual approved descriptions* from the handbook for administered assessments only. ## Required disclosure — exact text
> This report was developed with the assistance of AI-supported documentation tools to aid in the organization, documentation, and drafting of report content. All assessment procedures, clinical observations, analysis, interpretation of results and findings, professional conclusions, and recommendations reflect the independent clinical judgment of a licensed occupational therapist. This evaluation was completed, reviewed, and approved by the evaluating occupational therapist identified in this report.

## Publication checklist
Before calling any passage “AACPS-approved,” confirm the exact source, current version, intended circumstances, and permission to publish. Restricted district text belongs in an approved private source, not public GitHub.
